COMMANDER FORGE
===============

Build, score, rate and improve Magic: The Gathering Commander decks on your own computer.
Nothing to install: this folder carries its own copy of Node.js.

START
  macOS:   double-click "Commander Forge.command"
  Windows: double-click "Commander Forge.bat"

A window opens and your browser shows the app. Keep that window open while you use
Commander Forge. Close it, or press Ctrl+C in it, to stop.

FIRST RUN
  The first start downloads the card data and about 66,000 card images, roughly 5 GB.
  - Card data takes about 3 minutes. The app opens as soon as it is done.
  - Images keep downloading in the background for about 40 minutes. Cards show
    without pictures until theirs arrive. If you stop the app early, the download
    picks up where it left off next time.
  After that, Commander Forge works offline.

IF YOUR COMPUTER BLOCKS IT
  macOS ("cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer"):
    right-click "Commander Forge.command", choose Open, then Open again. Only needed once.
    Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/path/to/this/folder"
  Windows ("Windows protected your PC"):
    click "More info", then "Run anyway".

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Commander Forge
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Commander Forge
  Your decks, settings, card data and images are all there, not in this folder.

UPDATING
  Download the new zip from
  https://github.com/Egecan33/mtg-commander-deck-builder/releases/latest
  unzip it, and start it the same way. You can delete the old folder. Your data is kept.
  To refresh card data, use the Data page in the app.

The app only listens on 127.0.0.1, so other computers on your network cannot reach it.
